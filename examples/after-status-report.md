## What came out, and why

Source 799 words, output 258. **68 per cent shorter, and the two decisions moved from the last paragraph to the first line.**

| Pattern | What went |
|---|---|
| Explaining why you are writing | "I am writing to provide the steering group with an update..." |
| Structural narration | "This update covers... Before we begin, some context on how we got here." |
| Politeness padding | "I hope this finds you well", "Apologies for the length", "Happy to discuss at your convenience" |
| Background in front of the ask | The February approval, the operational review, the 40 per cent target. Real history, wrong position, and none of it needed to make the decision |
| The spelled-out consequence | "If these blockers are not resolved, we will not be able to proceed." The list said that |
| Didactic disclaimer | "It is important to note that delays at this stage tend to compound." |
| The hedged double-statement | "The overspend is not ideal, although it may be that the final position lands somewhat better." Kept the number, dropped the retreat |
| Defensive over-justification | An entire section defending the original scanner choice against an objection nobody raised. The group can't overturn a decision about a discontinued product, so the defence has no reader |
| Scope disclaimers at length | A paragraph listing four other programmes this update does not cover |
| Symmetry padding | Ashgrove, Hetherington and Marbury each got a paragraph to say nothing had changed. One table row now says it for all three |
| The recap | "Summary of the above", restating the document immediately before the ask |

**What stayed:** every figure (94.1%, 98.6%, £84,000, £610,000), the date (14 Nov), both requested decisions, all three blockers, all five sites, the July vacancy, the three-week Finance delay, and the pointer to the programme pack.

The February approval and the 40 per cent target were cut from the update, not from the record. They live in the business case, which is where a reader who wants them will look.
